About Malcolm Todd

Born and raised in Los Angeles, Malcolm Todd Hobert is an American singer-songwriter and producer crafting a unique blend of indie pop and 2010s alternative R&B. Raised in a creative family with rich musical influences ranging from Stevie Wonder to the Grateful Dead, Todd taught himself bass and guitar at age 16 during the COVID-19 pandemic. Incorporating lush bedroom-pop textures and distinctive chord progressions inspired by artists like Steve Lacy, Omar Apollo, and Dominic Fike, Todd has swiftly emerged as one of indie music’s most compelling new voices.

Early Beginnings & Breakthrough

Todd’s musical journey began in high school, writing, producing, and releasing his debut EP Demos Before Prom in 2022. After making the pivotal decision to forgo university to pursue music full-time, his momentum skyrocketed in 2023 when songs like "Art House" and "Roommates" went viral on TikTok. He made his live debut with a sold-out show in Venice, California, before releasing his breakout 2024 mixtape Sweet Boy and joining Omar Apollo as an opening act on the God Said No North American tour.

Major Releases & Commercial Success

In April 2025, Todd released his self-titled debut album Malcolm Todd, featuring the Billboard Hot 100 entry "Chest Pain (I Love)" and the Omar Apollo collaboration "Bleed." The expanded deluxe edition Malcolm Todd (still) reached a new peak at No. 49 on the Billboard 200 later that year. Continuing his rapid trajectory, Todd released his second studio album Do That Again in June 2026, alongside a massive resurgence of his 2024 fan-favorite track "Earrings," which climbed into the US Top 40 as a global sleeper hit.
